# Who sits at the desk

Yvonne Standish's regulars for Sprite Studio (skill `zx-game-ux`, §1), from spec §1 audience.

| Regular | Knows | Came to | Input | Gives up when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The sprite artist (Joo's desk) | squared paper, hex, frames, masks | draw and animate the cast quickly, see it at 1× | mouse at the desk, keys when the mouse is busy | a slip costs work, or the tool is slower than paper |
| A reader of the book | has read the drawing chapter; a little BASIC | try the chapter for themselves: rows as bytes, a second frame | keys or a joystick | they cannot find what the chapter describes |
| The newcomer at a show | nothing; no manual; five minutes; sound off | draw something and see it move | whatever is plugged in, usually keys | the first minute shows nothing they understand |
