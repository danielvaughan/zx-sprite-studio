# Playtest charter: Sprite Studio M2, round 2 (closing sit-beside trial)

- Tap: `build/sprite-studio-m2.tap`, sha256 `39bb8828a80634444ce5a8e1cf106463a5547b1b5c1a2d9c5a6ab2ebf0161c9b`
- Report: `playtest/m2-r2.md` (playtest report template, plus the tool-trial sections); screenshots
  `playtest/media/m2-r2-*`
- Method: Yvonne Standish's sit-beside trial (`zx-game-ux` §4). You are **a reader of the book**: you have read a
  chapter that draws a small lawn mower on squared paper, writes each row as two bytes in hex (row 1 is `$28`,
  `$00`), and makes it roll with a second frame. Keys only. You get **only the job cards**: no key list. Think
  aloud before each press; note hesitations and slips. 1987 words in the report.
- Speed: 25% or frame-step, stated. Agent-operated; full-speed feel not verified.

## Job cards

| Job | Goal (the reader's words) | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J1 | Find the two bytes the book gives for row 1 of the mower, on the screen | you read them off the screen and say where |
| J2 | Draw the second frame against the first, like tracing paper, then put the tracing paper away | the first frame shows faintly under the second; then not |
| J3 | Make the mower face the other way | it faces left |
| J4 | Slide the whole picture one square to the right | everything moved one square right |
| J5 | Find out whether anything is wrong with the picture, and look at the first problem | the problem's squares are shown |
| J6 | Make a frame that is almost the same as the one before it, see the warning, then change it until the warning goes | the warning appears, then goes |
| J7 | Copy a frame, change one square in the copy, and watch them play (as in the last trial) | the PLAY card shows the change |
| J8 | Take back the last change, then put it back | as before, then as after |

For each job: finished?, frames, presses, slips, hesitations, think-aloud lines.
