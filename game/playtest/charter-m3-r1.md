| Job | Goal (the reader's words) | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J1 | Put the gorilla from Tiger Gorilla on the desk to look at | the gorilla is on the squared paper |
| J2 | Say which game it comes from and how big its box is, from the screen | you read both off the screen and say where |
| J3 | Change one square on the gorilla, watch it run, then take the change back | the PLAY card shows the change; then it is gone |
| J4 | Go back to what was on the desk before the gorilla | the desk shows it again |
| J5 | Look round the studio's figures, then go back to the desk without taking any | the desk is as it was |
| J6 | Make the mower (from the studio's figures) face the other way in both its frames at once | both frames face left |
| J7 | Draw your own small figure, 8 dots by 8, with at least five squares inked | an 8 by 8 box with your squares |
| J8 | On a tall figure (Gold Master's Brian), find the two numbers for a row near his feet | you read them off the screen and say which row |

For each job: finished?, frames, presses, slips, hesitations, think-aloud lines.
