## Sit-beside trial (thinking aloud)

**Title** (`media/m2-r1-title.png`). "Choose your input. I am told keys only, so 2. It says the desk
opens on the mower: good, that's the book's mower."

**J1** (`media/m2-r1-desk.png`). "There's a HEX column beside the grid. The book says row 1 is `$28`,
`$00`. The line marked 1 says `2800`. That's both bytes stuck together, `28` then `00`, no dollar
signs. The top line is 0 and is blank, so the book's row 1 is the screen's line 1. Found it, no keys
needed." Hesitation: splitting `2800` into two bytes took a moment.

**J2** (`media/m2-r1-onion-on.png`, `media/m2-r1-cursor-onion.png`, `media/m2-r1-space-rubs.png`,
`media/m2-r1-onion-off.png`). "Tracing paper... I don't see those words. The blue list says FRAMES W
at the top: maybe W shows a different list." W gave the DRAWING list. "UNDO, ONION, MIRROR, NUDGE,
CHECK. Onion... onion skin, like the thin paper. That must be it. But first I need frame 2." W back,
X: "FRAME 2/2". W, N: "ONION SKIN ON. Little diamonds where frame 1's wheel squares were. Yes, that's
the first frame showing faintly." Moved onto what I thought was a diamond and pressed SPACE: "It went
WHITE. The bottom line says SPACE:INK but it says RUB OUT. I rubbed out a square I wanted." U put it
back (and the list now says REDO). One square right was a real diamond: SPACE inked it, ENTER rubbed
it again. N: "ONION SKIN OFF, the diamonds are gone. Paper put away."

**J3** (`media/m2-r1-mirror.png`, `media/m2-r1-mirror-both.png`). "MIRROR I." "The big picture faces
left... but the little frame-1 picture under the grid still points right. It only turned this frame."
W, Z, W, I: "Now both little pictures face left."

**J4** (`media/m2-r1-nudge.png`, `media/m2-r1-nudge-both.png`). "NUDGE > 8." "Moved right, nothing fell
off the edge, INKED stays 086. Same as mirror, it'll only be this frame." Tried X without changing
lists: "NEXT works from the DRAWING list too, so I didn't need W all those times." 8 again: "both
moved."

**J5** (`media/m2-r1-check.png`, `media/m2-r1-check-stray.png`, `media/m2-r1-check-mark.png`). "It already
says CHECKS OK. CHECK Y: NO PROBLEMS FOUND. So nothing is wrong. To see what a problem looks like I'll
put a lonely dot in the corner." CHECKS changed to 1 as soon as I inked it. Y: "STRAY DOT: FRAME 2,
and the square is checkered. Clear enough." Moved the cursor off it: the checkered square stands out on
its own.

**J6** (`media/m2-r1-copy.png`, `media/m2-r1-warn-same.png`, `media/m2-r1-warn-one.png`,
`media/m2-r1-warn-seven.png`). "COPY C: FRAME 3/3, CHECKS 1. Y: TOO ALIKE: FRAMES 2,3. But it has
taken me to frame 2. I want the copy, so X." One square changed, Y: "still TOO ALIKE, and back on
frame 2 again, with the one square crossed. How many does it want?" Six more changes: "seven crosses
and still too alike. Out of ninety squares. The screen gives no number." Kept adding squares in the
empty top row, watching CHECKS: "There, the tenth changed square, CHECKS OK."

**J7** (`media/m2-r1-j7-copy-change.png`, `media/m2-r1-play.gif`). "Same as last time: C, change one
square, G for PLAY. The PLAY card goes round four pictures; the line that said 0.08S OFF now says
0.08S ON. So that line was the playing speed." The PLAY card shows four different pictures, so the
copy with its one changed square is in the loop (the change is a single dot at true size).

**J8** (`media/m2-r1-undo.png`, `media/m2-r1-redo.png`). "UNDO U: UNDO, the dot is gone, and the list
says REDO. U again: REDO, the dot is back." One press each way.

## Cold start and tone

- Desk features first met (cold load, keys): the example mower, HEX column and true-size EDIT/PLAY
  cards at frame 49; the second list (DRAWING) on the first W; onion skin, mirror, nudge and check all
  listed there.
- Tone pass: done. Screens of text read: title (twice, including `YOUR PICTURES ARE KEPT: PRESS 1 OR 2
  TO CARRY ON.`), the desk, both lists, and every bottom-line message met (`INPUT CHOSEN`, `DRAWING
  CONTROLS`, `NEXT FRAME`, `PREVIOUS FRAME`, `ONION SKIN ON/OFF`, `INK`, `RUB OUT`, `UNDO`, `REDO`,
  `MIRRORED`, `NUDGED`, `NO PROBLEMS FOUND`, `STRAY DOT: FRAME 2`, `FRAME COPIED`, `TOO ALIKE: FRAMES
  2,3`, `PLAY`, `CAN'T: FIRST`). No real people's names or brands; the only name is the publisher line
  `© 2026 DALEX SOFTWARE`. Words are friendly and plain. The mower pictures read as a mower; no
  unwanted shapes seen at 1x.
