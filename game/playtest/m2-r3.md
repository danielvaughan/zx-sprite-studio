## Start screen picture at 1×

The harness starts from a snapshot taken after loading: at frame 2 the Start screen was already
up, so **the loading screen was not seen loading**. To look at it anyway the agent read the
6,912-byte screen block out of the tap and drew it: `media/m2-r3-loading-from-tap.png` (shown
at 2×). It is the same picture as the Start screen with `SPRITE STUDIO`, `SQUARED PAPER AND A
PENCIL` and the studio's mark.

Start screen (`media/m2-r3-start.png`, frame 102, 320×240 at 1×):

- Top half: a blue board holding a sheet of dotted squared paper with the mower drawn large on
  it, a yellow pencil with a white end and a red point lying along the paper's foot, and a white
  portable television with a black screen showing the mower at its true size, two yellow knobs
  and a speaker grille. All three pictures are whole: nothing is cut off, torn or out of place,
  and there is no colour clash at the edges between paper, pencil, board and set.
- Title `SPRITE STUDIO` in large yellow letters, readable at once.
- Menu below: `CHOOSE YOUR INPUT`, then `1 MOUSE` and `2 KEYS/JOYSTICK` in black on green bars,
  `THE DESK OPENS ON AN EXAMPLE, THE MOWER. CLR R CLEARS IT.`, `MUSIC ON (M)`, `PRESS 1 OR 2`,
  `© 2026 DALEX SOFTWARE`. All readable at 1×; nothing overlaps the picture.
- Real brand, logo or person: none, other than the studio's own mark (F-4). The television has
  no maker's name; the pencil has no name on it.
- Silhouette check at 1×: nothing reads as anything unwanted; the mower is the only shape that
  could be taken for something else (F-3), and the alternatives are harmless.
- Compared with round 2's title (`media/m2-r2-title.png`): the line `DRAW IT, FLIP IT, SEE IT AT
  THE SIZE THE PLAYER SEES IT` and the line `M: MUSIC ON OR OFF` are gone; `MUSIC ON (M)` still
  gives the key.
