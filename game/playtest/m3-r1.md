## Tone pass over the Library

All 103 Library entries were stepped through one by one with the true-size preview and panel read
(`media/m3-r1-library-tone-1.png`, `media/m3-r1-library-tone-2.png`, `media/m3-r1-library-tone-3.png`, `media/m3-r1-library-tone-4.png`; frames 10363 to 12343). Six games: Mower Mayhem,
Tiger Gorilla, Gold Master, Antigravity, All Disco, Wolf.

- Brand or product: `OMNIBOT` (F-1). The pose name `DIZZY` under All Disco's `DAN` is an ordinary word
  for a dance pose. The studio's own game names and its mark are allowed (TONE-01).
- Real people: the figures carry single given names only, no surnames. The agent cannot tell whether
  any of the character names (for example the two figures under All Disco's `HELPERS`) is based on a
  real person; for the owner to confirm (F-12). Faces (Brian, the Gold Master `ICONS SET 4` face, All Disco's
  dancers) are plain cartoon faces, not a likeness the agent recognises.
- Unkind content: none. Wolf's gym and body poses, Tiger Gorilla's SNAPPER with a camera, the croc,
  the jet and the drone are neutral.
- Silhouettes at 1x: nothing reads as a noose, gallows, weapon pointed at an animal or anything
  unwanted. `BEAM SET 3` is a diagonal line; `WOLF PROPS POSES 4` a single upright bar with nothing
  across it.
- Screens of text read: Start screen (first visit and after BACK), Desk (three list pages), Library;
  messages met: `INPUT CHOSEN`, `OPENED: <name>`, `RUB OUT`, `INK`, `PLAY`, `STOP`, `DRAWING CONTROLS`,
