# Playtest charter: Sprite Studio M7, round 1 (sit-beside trial, before the first counted verify)

- Tap: `build/sprite-studio-m7.tap` (check the loaded sha256 against `sha256sum` of the file before playing)
- Report: `playtest/m7-r1.md`; screenshots `playtest/media/m7-r1-*`
- Method: Yvonne Standish's sit-beside trial (`zx-game-ux` §4). You are **a reader who has just finished the
  drawing chapter** (rows of dots, frames and tracing paper, masks, putting a figure down three ways, why drawing
  flickers, colour belonging to the cell) and wants to see each idea happen. Keys first. You get **only the job
  cards**. Think aloud; 1987 words.
- Speed: 25% or frame-step, stated. No Kempston mouse and no tape deck in the harness: say so. Sound: the harness
  may let you hear or see the AY; say what you can tell (a click, a low blip, a chime) and what you cannot.

## Job cards

| Job | Goal (the reader's words) | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| J1 | Find the lessons from the chapter | you see the list |
| J2 | See how a sprite is rows of bytes, with the mower | you can read the mower's first row as bytes |
| J3 | See the mower's two frames moving with the tracing paper on, then draw on it | it plays, and your dot is there |
| J4 | See the three ways a game can put the mower down | you saw all three |
| J5 | See why drawing flickers | you can say which copy flickers and why |
| J6 | See colour clash happen | you can point at a clashing block |
| J7 | Get back to the figure you drew before the lessons | your own figure is there as you left it |
| J8 | Try each lesson in a different order from the first time | each shows what it says |

For each job: finished?, frames, presses, slips, hesitations, think-aloud lines.
