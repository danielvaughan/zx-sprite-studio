## What the operator said (think-aloud)

Most lines were written before the press they describe. The J1 opener, the J3 line about holding A, the
J5 BACK T line, the J6 DEL V line, the J7 plan and the J8 opener were set down at the time but polished
afterwards for this report. In J7 the operator read row 1 off the saved screen first, then confirmed it
with the cursor.

- Title: "It says PRESS 1 OR 2; I'm at a keyboard so 2 KEYS/JOYSTICK."
- J1: "Cursor is at SQUARE 6,8. I'll go right one with P to a square that looks white, then SPACE to paint,
  and watch INKED go up from 086." (The operator's misreading of a small view of the screen: the panel showed `8,8`, plainly drawn with a closed
  top loop, unlike a 6, and after P it read 9,8. Not a fault of the screen.)
  Then: "INKED 087 and the message says INK. Done."
- J2: "FRAME 1 OF 2. COPY C should copy this picture; I expect FRAME 2 OF 3 and a third little picture in
  the strip at the bottom." Then: "FRAME 2 OF 3 / FRAME COPIED, and the little arrows moved under the middle
  picture. Now I'll change one square: ENTER should rub out the ink under the cursor, and INKED should drop
  to 086." Then: "PLAY G... it says STOP G now, so G again should stop it."
- J3: "The bottom row is F. A should take me down; I'll hold A to see how the repeat behaves." Then:
  "Stopped on D, two more A. There's no LINE on the panel, so I'll try holding SPACE down while I tap P,
  like keeping a pen on the paper." (Hesitation: the screen does not say whether this works; it does.)
- J4: "I'll make a real slip, one square too many, then UNDO U should take back just that square. INKED
  should go 093 then back to 092." Side check: "Will U again take away the whole line?" It brought the
  slip back instead, with REDO on the message line (F-1).
- J5: "BACK T sounds like 'back to the first screen'. Then 2 for KEYS, and I hope I land on B,F with this
  picture." On the first screen: "YOUR PICTURES ARE KEPT: PRESS 1 OR 2 TO CARRY ON. That's reassuring."
- J6: "DEL V throws a picture away. Three now, so two presses of V should leave FRAME 1 OF 1. Then UNDO U
  to change my mind." Then: "The copy with my line went first. I was looking at it, so that's fair."
- Refusal: "Down to one picture, then V once more. I expect it to refuse because you can't have no
  pictures." It said `CAN'T: ONLY 1`: "Clear." Then: "Did that refused DEL spoil my undo?" It did not,
  and U brought the second picture back.
- J7: "I'm on FRAME 1. Row 1 is the second row. I'll read it against the 0-F heading, then check by
  walking the cursor along row 1, since the cursor changes to a dot on ink." (Hesitation: the panel shows
  SQUARE x,y but not whether that square is ink. The operator relied on the cursor's two shapes.)
  Answer: squares 2 and 4 (ink-dot cursor on 2,1 and 4,1, full cursor on paper at 3,1),
  `media/m1-r3-j7-col2.png`, `media/m1-r3-j7-col4.png`.
- J8: "FLIP 0.08S is how long each picture shows. G to play, then SLOW J should make the number bigger
  and the PLAY card slower, and FAST K the reverse." Then: "What happens at the fastest end?"
  The answer was `CAN'T: FASTEST`.

Score: ((4-1)+(4-1)+(5-1)+(4-1)+(4-1) + (5-2)+(5-2)+(5-2)+(5-2)+(5-1)) x 2.5 = (16 + 16) x 2.5 = **80 / 100**
(about 68 is average). Round 2 scored 77.5 (one agent tester, frame by frame).

What pulled it down:

- 4 and 2 (scored 2): the line trick (F-2) had to be guessed. A newcomer who does not guess has twice the
  presses.
- 6 (scored 2): G also changes on a second press, but its label shows it (PLAY G becomes STOP G). U is the
  only key whose label does not show what a second press will do (REDO, F-1).
- 8 (scored 2): long moves across the 16x16 sheet with the keys are slowish. Held-key repeat helps.
- 1, 3, 7, 9 (scored 4): no single fault. A first session left the operator sure, not delighted.

What held it up: every key announces itself on the message line (INK, RUB OUT, FRAME COPIED, UNDO,
FRAME DELETED, SLOWER, FASTER). Refusals say why (`CAN'T: ONLY 1`, `CAN'T: FASTEST`) and mark the key that
was refused. The first screen promises YOUR PICTURES ARE KEPT and keeps the promise. CLR R and DEL can both
be taken back with U (`media/m1-r3-clr.png`, `media/m1-r3-clr-undo.png`).

## Held-key repeat

Holding A for 30 frames from row 8: the first step comes at once, then a pause of 14 frames, then one
square every 4 frames. At 50 frames a second that is a pause of about 0.3 s, then 12.5 squares a second.
On the stepped emulator, the pause was long enough to make a single press move exactly one square, and
the repeat stopped on the row the operator released on (D) with no run-on. Holding Q for 80 frames and O
for 60 frames stopped at the top and left edges without wrapping round. Whether the pause and speed feel
right at full speed is not verified by the agent.

## The Grid Cursor

The cursor could always be seen. On paper it is a cyan square with a black edge (`media/m1-r3-j5-return.png`).
On ink it is a black square with a cyan centre and cyan edge (`media/m1-r3-j1-painted.png`,
`media/m1-r3-j7-col2.png`). Both shapes still read in grey, as a light grey square with a black edge and a
grey dot inside black. The difference between the two shapes is also the only on-screen way to tell
whether the square under the cursor is ink (J7 hesitation).
