- Charter: `playtest/charter-m1-r2.md`

Agent-operated. The first screen was watched running at 25% (`zx.speed(25)`); from frame 51 the
emulator was paused and every job was driven with `zx.tap`/`zx.step`, so frame counts are game
frames, not the operator's thinking time. The loaded tap's sha256 (`zx.status().tapSha256`) matched
the one above. Feel at full speed: not verified by the agent (for the user on The Spectrum).

Method: Yvonne Standish's sit-beside trial. The operator played the newcomer at a show: job cards
only, no manual, no key list, sound off, keys only (option 2 on the first screen; no mouse). The
operator did not read the design, the spec, the source, the user guide or the round-1 report.

### What the operator said, per job

- **First screen.** "No mouse here, so 2 for keys." (`1 MOUSE` / `2 KEYS/JOYSTICK` bars are plain.)

Lines in quotation marks were written at the time, before each press or as the screen answered;
plain text is what the screen showed.
- **J1.** "The bottom line says SPACE:PAINT and the side says SQUARE 08,08; the little blue box must
  be me, sitting on a white square. I'll press SPACE and expect it to go black." It did; INKED went
  086 → 087 and the message said `INK`.
- **J2.** "I need to copy the picture. The blue card has NEW C but no COPY. I'll try NEW and see
  whether it comes up blank or a copy." Then: "NEW gave me a copy, ink and all: FRAME 2 OF 3, the
  arrows moved to the middle picture." "ENTER says RUB, so I'll rub out the square I'm
  on." (`RUB OUT`, INKED 086.) "PLAY G should set it moving." The card changed to STOP G and `FLIP
  0.08S ON`; the PLAY box cycled three different pictures, four game frames each (checked over 40
  recorded frames). Drawing went on working with PLAY running.
- **J3.** "Bottom row: the paper is 16 squares, so row 15. A is down; I'll press A seven times and
  watch the SQUARE numbers." (`SQUARE 08,15`.) "First number across, second down. To draw a line
  I'll hold SPACE and tap P five times, hoping it paints as it goes." It did: squares 08-13 on row
  15, INKED 086 → 092.
- **J4.** "I'll make a mistake on purpose: up one with Q and paint. Then UNDO U should take just
  that square back." INKED 093 → 092, `UNDO`; the line stayed whole.
- **J5.** "BACK T should take me to the first screen. To come back I expect to press 2 again, but
  I'm worried that starts afresh." At the first screen: "Nothing says my picture is kept or how to
  carry on. I'll press 2 as before and hope." Everything was as left: same picture, SQUARE 13,14,
  FRAME 2 OF 3, PLAY still on.
- **J6.** "DEL V throws away a picture. I have three; two presses should leave one." (`FRAME
  DELETED` twice, then FRAME 1 OF 1.) "What if I press V with only one left? I expect a refusal."
  (`CAN'T: ONLY 1`, DEL V in red.) "Clear. Now change my mind: UNDO U should bring the last one
  back." FRAME 1 OF 2, INKED 087.
- **J7.** "I'm on FRAME 1 OF 2, so this is the first picture. Row 1 is the second row from the
  top. I'll count along it by eye, using the dotted squares as a ruler, then check by walking the
  cursor." Counting by eye at screen size did not work ("Hard to count at this size"); the operator
  then read the saved screen picture square by square: squares 2 and 4 are inked. "I'll check by
  walking the cursor to 02,01, O left eleven, Q up thirteen, and see the cursor sit on ink." At
  `SQUARE 02,01` the cursor showed the small (3×3) cyan centre of an inked square: confirmed. 04,01
  was not walked to.
- **J8.** "FLIP 0.08S looks like the time per picture. SLOW J should make it bigger; FAST K
  smaller." J: `FLIP 0.12S`, `SLOWER` (six game frames per picture). K twice: `FLIP 0.04S`,
  `FASTER` (two game frames per picture). One more K: `CAN'T: FASTEST`, FAST K in red.

### Ergonomics notes

- The key card on screen (blue, two columns) and the two-line hint at the bottom were enough for
  every job; no job needed a key that was not shown.
- Moving the cursor across the paper is one square per press; J7's check cost 24 presses. Held-key
  repeat was not tried.
- Every action answered at once with a one- or two-word message under the readouts.
