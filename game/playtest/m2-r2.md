## Sit-beside trial: what the reader said

- Title: "Choose your input. Keys only, so 2. It says the desk opens on the mower, good: that's the
  book's one."
- J1: "There's a HEX column beside the paper. The line labelled 1 says 28 00, white then yellow,
  same as the book. The paper starts at 0, so the book's row 1 is the second row. They agree."
- Looking around: "The blue list says FRAMES W at the top. Press W... now it's DRAWING: UNDO,
  TRACING, MIRROR, NUDGE, CHECK. W turns the page."
- J2: "Tracing paper is TRACING, N. But I need frame 2 first. NEXT was X on the other page; does X
  work while this page shows? ... Yes, FRAME 2/2." / "N. Little rings where frame 1's ink is.
  Faint, like tracing paper." / "I'll ink the ring at 2,1 so I've drawn something: up seven, left
  six, SPACE. The cursor sits round the ring; I can still see the ring inside it." / "N again,
  TRACING PAPER OFF, rings gone."
- J3: "MIRROR, I. 'Mirrored this frame.' The strip at the bottom: frame 1 still faces right. The
  mower is both frames, so Z back and I again. Now both face left."
- J4: "NUDGE > is 8. Nothing fell off the edge, INKED is the same. Same again: only this frame. X,
  8."
- J5: "The corner already says CHECKS OK. Y: 'No problems found.' Nothing wrong with it, then. The
  mistake I'd make on squared paper is a stray dot, so I'll ink a lone one. Y: 'STRAY DOT: FRAME 2',
  and the square goes chequered. That's it shown."
- J6: "COPY gives me a twin: frame 3. CHECKS has gone to 1. Y: 'TOO ALIKE 2,3: 10 MORE'. Ten more
  squares different, I think." / "Five inked: 05 MORE. It's counting down, good." / "Nine:
  01 MORE. One more... that's ten. Still 01 MORE? Y again. Still 01. It said ten." / "Eleven.
  'No problems found.' It wanted eleven, not ten."
- J7: "COPY, C, frame 4. One square, D,0. PLAY is G. The PLAY card is going round four pictures; the
  last one has two dots at the top where the one before has one. That's my change."
- J8: "UNDO, U. The square's gone and the list now says REDO U. So U again... back. Easy."
- Refusal: "On frame 1, PREV again. 'CAN'T: FIRST FRAME' and PREV flashes white then black. Fair."
- BACK: "T goes to the title: 'YOUR PICTURES ARE KEPT: PRESS 1 OR 2 TO CARRY ON.' Press 2: all four
  frames still there and still playing."

## Cold start and tone

- Headline jobs first met (cold load, no level select): the hex for each row on the desk's first
  screen (frame 360); tracing paper, mirror, nudge, check and undo on the DRAWING list one W away;
  frames, copy and play on the first list.
- Tone pass: done, 3 screens of text read (title, the title after BACK, the desk with both lists)
  and 20 messages (`INPUT CHOSEN`, `DRAWING CONTROLS`, `NEXT FRAME`, `TRACING PAPER ON/OFF`, `INK`,
  `MIRRORED THIS FRAME`, `NUDGED THIS FRAME`, `NO PROBLEMS FOUND`, `STRAY DOT: FRAME 2`,
  `FRAME COPIED`, `TOO ALIKE 2,3: nn MORE`, `PLAY`, `UNDO`, `REDO`, `CAN'T: FIRST FRAME`,
  `YOUR PICTURES ARE KEPT: PRESS 1 OR 2 TO CARRY ON.`). No real names; the only name is the
  studio's own (`© 2026 DALEX SOFTWARE`). The words are short and friendly; the refusal says what
  is wrong, not that the operator is wrong. No breaches.
- Silhouette check at 1×: the mower sprite and the strip pictures read as a mower; nothing reads as
  anything unwanted.
