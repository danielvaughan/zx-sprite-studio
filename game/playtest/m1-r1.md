## Cold start and tone

- Headline features first met (cold load, no level select):
  - Draw square by square: Desk, first visit; `2` pressed at frame ~140, Desk shown with the cursor on 08,08 by frame 181 (`media/m1-r1-desk-first.png`); first square inked at frame ~880.
  - True-size preview: Desk, frame 181, updates in the same few frames as the Grid (`media/m1-r1-paint-ink.png`).
  - Frames and flip-book: Desk, frame 181: the strip shows the mower's two frames and "FRAME 1 OF 2"; the flip-book first played at frame ~1557 (`media/m1-r1-play.gif`).
  - Masks, clash, Row Codes, lessons: not met and not signposted on the M1 Desk, as the charter and D-010 expect for this milestone.
- The loading screen was not seen: the harness starts from the tape-loader snapshot and frame 20 already shows the Start screen. Bot checks `house_loader` and `house_logo` cover it.
- Tone pass: done, 2 screens and the readouts captured in screenshots. Text read: "SPRITE STUDIO", "SQUARED PAPER AND A PENCIL", "CHOOSE YOUR INPUT", "1 MOUSE", "2 KEYS/JOYSTICK", "DRAW IT, FLIP IT, SEE IT AT THE SIZE THE PLAYER SEES IT", "1 2: INPUT  M: MUSIC", "MUSIC ON (M)" / "MUSIC OFF (M)", "PRESS 1 OR 2", "© 2026 DALEX SOFTWARE" (house footer); Desk: "MOWER 16X16", "TRUE SIZE", "EDIT", "FLIP-BOOK", "INPUT KEYS"/"INPUT MOUSE", "SQUARE nn,nn", "FRAME n OF n", "INKED nnn", "FLIP 0.08S OFF/ON", events seen "INPUT CHOSEN", "INK", "RUB OUT", "UNDO", "REDO", "NEXT FRAME", "NEW FRAME", "FRAME DELETED", "FRAME CLEARED", "PLAY", "SLOWER" (the event lines for previous frame, stop and faster were not captured), refusals "CAN'T: LAST", "CAN'T: FIRST", "CAN'T: 8 MAX", "CAN'T: ONLY 1", "CAN'T: FASTEST", "CAN'T: SLOWEST", "CAN'T: NO UNDO"; controls "PREV Z, NEXT X, NEW C, DEL V, CLR R, UNDO U, PLAY/STOP G, BACK T, SLOW J, FAST K"; legend "QAOP:MOVE SPACE:PAINT ENTER:RUB", "1:MOUSE 2:KEYS". No real person, brand, product or existing game named (Dalex is the house label); refusals state the limit, never blame; text plain and friendly. No breach.
- Silhouette check at 1x: the mower (Grid and both cards) reads as a small wheeled machine seen from above (body, rounded front, four wheel marks); the Arrow reads as an arrow. No unintended reading found.
